<p class="intro-name"><strong>Ashutosh Shukla</strong></p>
<p class="tagline">Applied Scientist · Robotics Planning at Atoms · Ph.D., Operations Research, UT Austin</p>
<p>My work applies operations research to decision problems in physical systems, with applications in mining, rail transportation, and electric power infrastructure. I am currently a Member of Technical Staff in Robotics Planning at Atoms, where I work on motion planning, coordination, and fleet planning for autonomous haul trucks in mines and quarries.</p>
<p>Before that, I was at BNSF Railway, where I led a team of five that built a decision support system for flat yard switching in rail terminals. The system is now in use at 11 yards, and a patent application has been filed for the work. I also built the optimization model that assigns trains to fueling pads at BNSF's largest fueling terminal.</p>
<p>I received my Ph.D. from the University of Texas at Austin. My dissertation studied how to make power grids resilient to rare, extreme flood events. It developed stochastic optimization models for allocating budgets for infrastructure hardening and emergency preparedness, along with methods for generating flood risk scenarios. A paper from this research received the Best Student Paper Award at the IISE Annual Conference. My methods include stochastic optimization, simulation, and reinforcement learning.</p>
