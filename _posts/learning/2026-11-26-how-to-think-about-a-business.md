Today, humans spend way more time on their phone than anywhere else. Therefore it is reasonable to say that they derive maximum utility from it. So to maximize wealth, we need to provide something to humans on their phone such that they spend the most amount of time engaging with it. Therefore, not surprisingly, the richest companies are those whose apps appear on the home screen of most phones. One other way to make wealth, if you are not making any such app, is to enable people to get more of that screen time: for example, by providing domestic services, healthcare, groceries, or anything else at scale. Basically, we want to cut down on inefficiency for the largest number of humans so that they get more time to do creative tasks, or to keep themselves entertained by consuming that screen time.

## Business analysis

There are three main sheets to look at while assessing the financial performance of a company:

- **Profit and loss statement:** reflects the movement of product
- **Balance sheet:** use of assets for the operation of the company
- **Cash flow statement:** movement of cash as a result of the company's activities
